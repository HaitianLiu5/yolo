# Agent Note: Yolo 1.0 and the path to it

Status: proposed

English | [中文](2026-10-11-yolo-1-0.zh.md)

## Problem

Yolo does a lot: an agent deploys and upgrades an instance ([agent deployment](../../implemented/feature/2026-10-13-agent-deployment.md)), media goes from the node to the screen by a direct path ([media off the Worker](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-13-media-off-the-worker.md)), one player plays in the car and in any browser ([two engines](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-12-two-playback-engines.md)), agents drive it over MCP and from the screen ([remote MCP server](../../implemented/feature/2026-10-07-remote-mcp-server.md), [agent from the screen](../../implemented/feature/2026-10-18-agent-from-the-screen.md)), and items have danmaku, AI subtitles, metadata, and runs that play on ([up next](../../implemented/feature/2026-10-23-up-next.md)).

The flow a person actually follows is broken at both ends. A new owner who pairs a screen without Plex sees an empty home screen with nothing to say what to do. A single web video with no run and an empty queue ends on "Finished", and nothing plays next. In between, a second screen that starts playing stops the first, and some failures end on an overlay without a button.

The earlier definition of 1.0 counted deployment, media paths, sources, and the plugin API, for the car and the web only, with "one playing at a time" as its usage model. It closed no scope around the person in front of the screen, so work spread across directions while the main flow stayed open. The defaults also fit the environment Yolo grew up in, Plex on the LAN, a proxy into mainland China, a signed-in Bilibili account, and a Tesla, rather than a new owner who has one of those or none.

## Proposal

### What 1.0 is

1.0 is the main loop, on every 1.0 client and across them: open Yolo and find something to watch, watch it, have something next when it ends, and keep watching, on this screen or another.

```
 open ──▶ something to watch ──▶ watch ──▶ it ends ──▶ something next ──┐
  ▲          (home rows,           (no dead     (series, queue,          │
  │           cold start)           ends)        uploader, follows,      │
  │                                              agent)                  │
  └──────────── another screen takes over, with the same progress ◀──────┘
```

### Clients

| Client | At 1.0 |
|---|---|
| Web player | The car profile (canvas engine) in the Tesla browser, and the web profile (`<video>` engine) in any other browser |
| Apple TV | The native tvOS app ([Apple TV client](2026-10-19-apple-tv-client.md)) |
| iOS | The native iPhone and iPad app ([#72](https://github.com/HaitianLiu5/yolo/pull/72)), on the same client protocol |

There is no Android client: Android phones run the web player in the browser, and Android TV can run it in a WebView. The Samsung TV app (the [TV web client](2026-10-26-tv-web-client.md)) opens the same web player and is no 1.0 criterion. Agents are not a client: ChatGPT, Claude, and Codex reach Yolo over MCP, and a screen's Ask hands a request to the owner's own agent at home.

### Five promises

1. **Something to watch when it opens.** The home screen is made of the owner's own sources: follows, followed series, and watch later on the video sites they use, Plex, and folders at home. With nothing connected, it says how to connect a source.
2. **Something next when it ends.** The next in the series, then the queue, then the next from the same uploader or channel, then new uploads from follows, then the agent's pick.
3. **Every screen plays its own, and any screen takes over another with one press.** Progress and watched state are shared by all of them.
4. **No dead ends.** Every failure says what happened and offers a next step.
5. **A press plays at once, and a break picks up where it was.** Caching and prefetch come last of the five.

### Experience layers

| Layer | What the owner has | What Yolo gives |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | No source connected; the owner asks an agent and the screen plays it | Still usable: after an item ends the agent picks the next, and the home screen shows history and what agents played |
| Normal, the 1.0 target | Their own sources connected | Home rows from those sources; the owner finds and presses things themselves, and the agent helps |
| Best | Several sources, several screens | One-press take-over between screens and a press that plays at once |

A screen that plays only what an agent sends is the floor, never what Yolo is for. The cold start and the home screen move the owner from the floor to the normal layer; asking the agent is the fallback they offer last, not the first choice.

### Design principles

- **Designed for a new owner, with a choice.** No plugin is installed on the owner's behalf; they choose the sources and extras they use.
- **Sign-in is never forced.** Yolo works without signing in to any site; signing in only makes it better, such as higher quality or the owner's follows.
- **Every step can be skipped** and turned on later, from any screen or by the owner's agent.
- **No dependency on one site.** An owner who uses only YouTube, only Plex, or only a folder at home reaches the normal layer. The main flow (home, what plays next, multi-screen) assumes no site and needs no proxy into mainland China.
- **Web-link playback is built in.** The yt-dlp source is always loaded: the floor depends on playing any link an agent hands over, and it is the source that claims arbitrary web links. In code it stays a plugin under `plugins/`, so the core names no site or tool. The home cold-start proposal ([#116](https://github.com/HaitianLiu5/yolo/pull/116)) replaces the earlier plan that took yt-dlp out of the default list and offered it at setup.

### The main flow, scene by scene

The "now" column was read from the code on 2026-10-31, not checked on devices.

| Scene | Now | At 1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| A. First open | After pairing, a new owner without Plex sees an empty home screen with no guidance | The home screen asks where the owner usually watches: the video sites, Plex, a folder at home, and last, asking the agent. Each choice turns on its plugin and connects it (a QR sign-in, a Plex address, a folder) and its rows appear at once. The question can be skipped, every screen remembers the skip, and a quiet entry stays on the home screen to come back to. The home cold-start proposal (`2026-10-31-home-cold-start.md`, [#116](https://github.com/HaitianLiu5/yolo/pull/116)) owns this |
| B. Every day | Continue Watching, the queue, playlists, the first movie library (at most 200 items), and the Plex shows | Rows from every connected source: new uploads from follows, followed series' next episodes, and watch later from profile plugins ([#110](https://github.com/HaitianLiu5/yolo/pull/110)), every Plex library with paging, and folders. When the node is offline, the home screen says so and what still works instead of dropping rows |
| C. Choosing | The web player shows a details sheet before playing; a TV press plays at once | Every client shows what an item is (synopsis, episodes, where to resume) before it plays, and Continue Watching still plays with one press |
| D. Watching | Some failures end on an overlay with no button and swallow taps: a subtitle or audio switch that fails, a canvas pick that fails, home, episodes, or resume that fail to load; a share page offers an audio menu that leads nowhere | Every failure says what happened and offers retry, close, or a way back to something that works |
| E. It ends | A run (a show's episodes, a video's parts, a playlist) plays on; a single video with no run and an empty queue stops on "Finished" | The continuation chain of promise 2, and an agent can set what plays next. The continuation-chain proposal owns the order and the candidates, drawing on the profiles' candidates from #110 |
| F. Other screens and family | One screen plays at a time: a screen that starts playing deactivates the one before it, and a tap in a browser plays on whichever screen is playing | Every screen has its own session; a press plays on that screen; any screen takes over another's item, position, and tracks with one press from its home screen; a screen marked as the household's keeps its plays out of the owner's history. The multi-screen proposal owns this |
| G. Telling the agent | The agent does not know what the owner follows elsewhere, and after what it played ends nothing follows | The agent reads the owner's profiles (#110), can set what plays next, and is the last link of the chain |

### Acceptance by client

Read from the code on 2026-10-31, not verified on devices.

**Web player, car and web profiles.** Every scene above can be walked on the web player today, within its "now" column. Gaps:

- The overlay `showStatus()` raises has no button: on a failed subtitle or audio switch, a failed canvas pick, and a failed home load.
- Only the first movie library shows, at most 200 items.
- Only an agent can reorder the queue or edit a playlist.
- When the node is offline, the home screen's library rows disappear without a word.
- Sharing in the car shows no QR code.
- No preferred subtitle language, no way to mark an item watched, and no manual quality.

**Apple TV.** Missing: the details screen; the up-next card and the end screen; error messages with retry; the queue and playlists; remote control of other screens and moving playback; the microphone for Ask; the agent canvas, being built on a branch; preferences; and the version on screen.

**iOS (#72).** Missing: Ask; the agent canvas; remote control of other screens; the queue; a share entry; sharing into Yolo from other apps; failure messages; and the end screen. Video may pause when the phone locks, which needs a check on a device.

### Three single-source owners

Each of three new owners, with one source and nothing else, walks the whole main loop on every 1.0 client and reaches the normal layer, with no Bilibili account, no proxy into mainland China, and no plugin they did not choose:

| Owner | Connects | The loop |
|---|---|---|
| Only YouTube | The YouTube profile, by its sign-in | The home screen shows their subscriptions' uploads and watch later; an upload plays from a press; when it ends the next from the same channel or another follow plays; another screen takes over |
| Only Plex | Their Plex server's address and sign-in | Every library shows with paging; an episode plays and the season goes on; a film ends and the next pick follows; another screen takes over |
| Only a folder at home | A folder on the node's machine or a mounted NAS share | Films and series show with posters and synopses; a file that needs a remux and one that needs a transcode play with seeking and resume; series go on; another screen takes over |

### The plugin system: 1.0 and 1.x

The home cold-start proposal ([#116](https://github.com/HaitianLiu5/yolo/pull/116)) owns the plugin system; this is its split.

At 1.0:

- A new node loads only built-in web-link playback. Every other plugin is chosen by the owner and turned on, set up, and turned off from the cold start, Settings › Plugins, the home machine's terminal (`npm run yolo -- plugins`), or MCP, where an agent's request to turn a plugin on waits for the owner's passkey.
- `@useyolo/plugin-api` stays experimental in the repository; its manifest carries what the catalog needs, such as the plugin's kinds, description, options' schema, and the hosts it talks to.
- A plugin with a control entry applies after a redeploy from the home machine, which the screen's agent can run.
- The owner's agent can build a local plugin; it is checked, approved with a passkey, loaded, and rolled back like any other, with a plain warning that it runs with the node's rights.
- Tencent Sports becomes its own plugin, `source-tencent-sports`, so built-in link playback carries no sign-in and no choice.
- Plugins get event hooks for plays, progress, and endings, which the continuation chain uses.
- An existing instance keeps the plugins it loads on upgrade; the release's `yolo-upgrade` block says so.

After 1.0, in 1.x: `@useyolo/plugin-api` published to npm under semver; a community index and `yolo install` at pinned versions; node plugins that register MCP tools themselves, so only DNS providers keep control entries; and isolation: each plugin in its own process, declared hosts enforced through the [egress rules](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-13-node-egress-proxy.md), and only declared environment variables, never `NODE_SECRET`. 1.0's design leaves room for all of it.

### The base, kept from the earlier definition

| Area | At 1.0 |
|---|---|
| Deploy | An owner with a Cloudflare account, with or without a domain, has their agent deploy Yolo from a tagged release with the shipped skills, `yolo doctor`, and the ignored instance file, and later upgrade and roll it back from the changelog, acting only at the checkpoints the skills list |
| Media | The Worker carries control; media takes the first direct path that works, LAN direct play then remote direct play, and the relay carries a screen's media only when none works, unless the owner turned it off ([media off the Worker](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-13-media-off-the-worker.md)) |
| Security | The blockers stay fixed with regression tests, unauthenticated routes are rate-limited, and the internet-facing surfaces have a recorded security review |
| Repository | CI is green, every package carries the release's version, no tracked file carries an owner's values, and the contributor guide takes a newcomer to a playing browser |

### Open items, for the owner to confirm

These items of the earlier definition move to 1.x unless the owner keeps them in 1.0:

- A WebDAV source.
- A Docker image of the node.
- [Distribution to other owners](2026-10-11-distribution-to-other-owners.md): installers, a setup wizard, `yolo install`, and backup and restore of a whole instance.
- A catalog in D1: browsing while the node is offline, and one item mapped to its copies on several sources so they share one history.
- A direct-URL source that plays `.mp4`, `.m3u8`, and `.mpd` links without yt-dlp; with web-link playback built in, yt-dlp already plays such links.
- Publishing `@useyolo/plugin-api` to npm (listed above under 1.x).
- Alerts when the node goes offline or a certificate fails to renew, and the security findings beyond the review's blockers: device impersonation on the realtime channel, the pairing lockout, a content security policy, an audit trail, and rotating keys.

### Out of 1.0

Android; music, podcasts, and audiobooks; accounts for several people and shared libraries (a household screen is not an account, and [share links](../../implemented/feature/2026-10-20-share-links.md) play one item for a friend); [self-hosting without Cloudflare](../architecture/2026-10-07-self-hosting-without-cloudflare.md); a Jellyfin-compatible protocol; live TV and IPTV; an SMB or NFS client inside the node; Yolo's own follows and an inbox beyond the profiles' lists; scheduled recommendations by the owner's agent; notifications; hedged reads over [egress routes](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-13-node-egress-proxy.md); and R2 staging for owners who cannot forward a port.

## Alternatives considered

**Keep the capability definition of 1.0.** Deployment, media paths, and the plugin API were close to done while a new owner still met an empty home and a video that stopped at its end. A person judges Yolo by the loop, so 1.0 is defined by it and keeps the capabilities as its base.

**An agent-first 1.0, where the screen plays what agents send.** It is the floor: a screen that only an agent can drive is useless when the agent is unavailable or the person does not know what to ask, and it leaves the home screen empty. The agent stays a helper and the last link of the chain.

**Load every official plugin by default.** A new owner would get sites they never use, sign-in prompts, and code running on their machine for nothing; the owner chooses, and web-link playback, which the floor needs, is the one plugin always loaded.

**Keep yt-dlp optional** ([#116](https://github.com/HaitianLiu5/yolo/pull/116) replaces that plan). Without it, a link an agent hands over plays only if another source claims it, and the floor breaks. It stays a plugin in code, so the core still names no tool.

**Native apps after 1.0.** People watch on the living-room TV and the phone in their hand, and the main flow has to hold there; the [client protocol](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-19-client-protocol.md)'s server side has shipped, so Apple TV and iOS need client work only. Android is left out because the web player already runs in its browser and WebView.

**A Jellyfin protocol plugin for Apple devices.** [Rejected](../../rejected/feature/2026-10-11-jellyfin-protocol-plugin.md): third-party players take no remote commands and show none of Yolo's own features; the Apple clients speak Yolo's protocol.

**Emulating a Plex server for third-party clients.** [Rejected](../../rejected/architecture/2026-10-11-emulate-a-plex-media-server.md): plex.tv's claim mechanism and Plex's terms do not allow it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Three single-source owners reach the normal layer.** The YouTube-only, Plex-only, and folder-only owners above each walk the whole loop on the web player in both profiles, on Apple TV, and on iOS, from pairing, with no plugin they did not choose, no Bilibili account, and no proxy into mainland China.
2. **The floor holds.** An owner with no source connected has an agent play a web link on a screen; when it ends, the agent's pick plays; the home screen shows history and what agents played, and offers the cold start again.
3. **Every scene meets its 1.0 column** on every 1.0 client, and the gap lists under acceptance by client are closed and checked on a device.
4. **The cold start is optional.** Skipping it on one screen is remembered by every screen; no sign-in is required to play; a source can be connected later from any screen or by the owner's agent.
5. **No site in the main flow.** No file under `service/` or `clients/` names a site, `scripts/provider-names.test.js` passes, and with only Plex or only a folder loaded, no request leaves for a video site.
6. **An existing instance is unchanged by the upgrade.** It loads the same plugins after the upgrade as before, as its `yolo-upgrade` block says.
7. **The base holds.** A friend's agent deploys from a fresh clone at the 1.0 tag and later upgrades and rolls back; the car on LTE plays `hls`, `direct`, `dash_split`, and `plex` streams by remote direct play wherever a direct path works; the security criteria and the repository criteria above hold.

## Risks

| # | Assumption | If it fails | Who checks, when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sites keep the sign-ins and lists the profile plugins read | A site's rows go stale or ask the owner to connect again; the loop falls back to the other links of the chain | The profile plugins' recorded-answer tests, and `yolo doctor` |
| 2 | Per-screen sessions fit the Hub and one protocol raise across three clients | A raise of `CLIENT_PROTOCOL` reaches TVs and phones that update separately; old clients keep the one-session behavior until updated | The multi-screen proposal, before its implementation starts |
| 3 | App Review accepts the Apple clients for other owners (guidelines 2.1 and 5.2.3) | They install through TestFlight on the owner's own devices only | Before the first App Store submission |
| 4 | iOS keeps video playing while the phone is locked or the app is in the background | Locking the phone stops the video, and iOS is not accepted until the client handles it | On a device, before iOS is accepted |
| 5 | Most owners can forward a port to the node | Every screen away from home plays through the relay, at its 4.8 Mbps ceiling and on the owner's Workers requests; R2 staging moves up | The relay's stream counts in settings and `doctor` |
| 6 | Workers VPC stays available at a price that fits, and Cloudflare's terms stay as they read on 2026-10-14 | `NODE_TRANSPORT=tunnel` without code changes; the media note, the relay's default, and the README follow new terms | When Cloudflare announces pricing or updates its terms, and before 1.0 |
| 7 | The scope stays closed until the main flow works | New directions compete with the loop again | Every proposal checks itself against this note's scenes before it starts |

Given up for 1.0: the items under "Out of 1.0", the open items that move to 1.x, and plugins from third parties beyond what the owner or their agent puts on their own node.
