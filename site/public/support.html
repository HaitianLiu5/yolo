How can we help?

Help for every Yolo app: the player in a car or any browser, Yolo TV for Apple TV, and the apps that come next.

Set up Yolo On your Cloudflare account and a machine at home. Pair a screen A car, a browser, or an Apple TV, once each. Get in touch GitHub Issues or email.

Set up Yolo.

Yolo is self-hosted. Its apps carry no content and play from a Yolo you run yourself. You need:

A Cloudflare account
The free Workers plan covers a household.
A machine at home
macOS or Linux, left on, with Node.js 22.12 or newer, yt-dlp, ffmpeg, and Deno. It runs the media node, which reads your media.
A domain, if you like
Without one, Yolo runs on your account's free workers.dev address.

Let an agent do it

Clone the repository on that machine, open it in Claude Code or Codex, and ask it to deploy Yolo. It follows the yolo-deploy skill and stops only for what is yours to do:

  1. Sign in to Cloudflare from any device, with a link and a code.
  2. Approve the plan: the resources it creates, and your address.
  3. Create your passkey at https://<your address>/login with the 6-digit code from npm run owner-code.
  4. Pair your first screen, and connect an agent if you wish.

To upgrade later, ask it to follow yolo-upgrade. To check on Yolo at any time, run npm run yolo -- doctor; anything wrong comes with its fix.

Pair a screen.

Each screen pairs once, approved with your passkey. A pairing code lasts 10 minutes and works once.

A car or any browser

  1. Open your Yolo address. It shows a QR code.
  2. Scan it with your phone and sign in with your passkey.
  3. Check the name and type, then tap Allow.

Apple TV

  1. Open Yolo TV at home. Allow local network access if asked.
  2. Confirm the server it finds, or type your address.
  3. Scan the QR code, sign in, and tap Allow.

Already signed in on a phone or computer? Its own page can be approved with a single tap.

Questions.

Does Yolo TV work without my own server?

No. Like other apps for media servers, Yolo TV plays from a server you run. It carries no content of its own.

Yolo TV doesn't find my server.

Make sure the Apple TV and your home machine share a network and that the media node is running (npm run yolo -- doctor). If you declined local network access, allow it in the Apple TV's Settings, or simply type your address. Away from home, typing it is the way.

The pairing code expired.

Codes last 10 minutes. The screen shows a new one; scan that.

Nothing plays, or it keeps buffering.

The media node must be on and online. Run npm run yolo -- doctor on your home machine and follow the fix for anything that fails. At home, video comes straight from the node; away, by its remote address or, failing that, through your Cloudflare account. On a slow connection the player lowers quality on its own.

Where is Ask, or its microphone?

Ask appears once an agent plugin, such as Claude Code or Codex, runs on your media node. The player's microphone appears when the node also has a transcription plugin. On Apple TV, dictate with the Siri Remote.

I lost a device.

Remove it in Yolo's settings, or ask your agent to. Its credential stops working at once.

I lost my passkey.

Run npm run owner-code on your home machine and sign in at https://<your address>/login within 15 minutes. The new passkey replaces all others.

How do I share a video with a friend?

Tap share in the player, or ask your agent. The link plays that one video in any browser for 7 days, and you can revoke it in Settings, under Share links.

Does Yolo collect my data?

No. Everything stays in your Cloudflare account and on your machine. Read the privacy policy.
