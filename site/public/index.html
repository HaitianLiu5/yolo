Your videos, played by your agents.

Yolo is a private, self-hosted media service. Your AI agents search, play, and control your videos on a Tesla's screen, an Apple TV, or any browser, streamed from your own home machine and your own Cloudflare account.

Clients

  • The player, in any browser, with a car profile built for a Tesla's browser.
  • Yolo TV for Apple TV.

Get started

Yolo is open source. Deploy it to your own account by following the support guide, or read the code at github.com/HaitianLiu5/yolo.
